Dr. Suhail A. Syed is the Founder and Lead Dentist at Redesign Dental Clinics, Banjara Hills, Hyderabad. With over 20 years of experience, he is committed to delivering modern, precise and patient-focused dental care.

His approach combines clinical expertise with a strong focus on aesthetics, comfort and long-term oral health. Dr. Suhail believes that exceptional dentistry goes beyond treatment — it is about understanding every patient, earning their trust and creating a comfortable and personalised treatment experience.

At Redesign Dental Clinics, every smile is treated with precision, care and a commitment to helping patients achieve healthier, confident and long-lasting smiles.
