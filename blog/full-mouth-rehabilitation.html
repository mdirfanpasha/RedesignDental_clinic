Understanding Full Mouth Rehabilitation

A comprehensive guide to multi-disciplinary full mouth restoration combining implants, crowns, periodontal therapy, and bite alignment.

RC
Redesign Dental Clinics
Clinical Editorial Team • Banjara Hills, Hyderabad

Full mouth rehabilitation (also known as full mouth reconstruction) is an individualized, comprehensive treatment plan that restores the health, function, and aesthetics of all teeth across the upper and lower jaws.

Key Clinical Takeaways

Full mouth rehabilitation restores optimal bite harmony, chewing comfort, and smile aesthetics through a structured, multi-specialty clinical approach.

Who May Benefit from Full Mouth Rehabilitation?

Patients with widespread tooth loss, severe enamel wear from bruxism (teeth grinding), extensive dental decay, collapsed bite vertical dimension, or severe periodontal damage benefit from comprehensive rehabilitation.

Combining Dental Specialties

Full mouth reconstruction integrates multiple disciplines, including prosthodontics (crowns, bridges, implants), periodontics (gum and bone stabilization), endodontics (root canal therapy), and orthodontics (bite realignment).

Diagnostic Planning & Digital Smile Design

Treatment begins with comprehensive records: 3D CBCT imaging, digital jaw movement analysis, facial photographic scans, and diagnostic wax-ups. This ensures the reconstructed bite functions in harmony with the temporomandibular joint (TMJ).

The Phased Treatment Journey

Care is carried out in methodical stages: initial disease control, provisional restorations to test the new bite and chewing comfort, and final placement of long-lasting ceramic and implant restorations.
