Clear and Invisible Braces: What Patients Should Know

How clear aligners work, suitability assessment, daily compliance (20-22 hours/day), and retention after treatment.

RC
Redesign Dental Clinics
Clinical Editorial Team • Banjara Hills, Hyderabad

Clear aligners offer a discreet, removable, and comfortable alternative to traditional metal brackets for correcting dental alignment and mild-to-moderate malocclusion.

Key Clinical Takeaways

Clear aligners offer effective and discreet tooth straightening when supported by consistent daily wear and dedicated retainer maintenance.

How Clear Aligners Move Teeth

Using advanced 3D digital scans, a series of custom-molded medical-grade polyurethane trays are fabricated. Each sequential aligner applies gentle, controlled biomechanical forces to guide teeth into their planned positions.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Clear Aligners?

Clear braces excel at treating crowded teeth, spacing issues, mild overbites, and minor bite relapse. Severe skeletal bite discrepancies or complex rotations may require specialized orthodontic evaluation.

Daily Routine and Compliance Requirements

Aligners must be worn for 20 to 22 hours per day, removed only for eating, drinking non-water beverages, and daily oral hygiene. High patient compliance is essential for achieving treatment timelines.

The Critical Role of Post-Treatment Retainers

After active tooth movement is complete, bone and periodontal ligaments require time to stabilize. Wearing custom retainers as directed by your orthodontist prevents unwanted orthodontic relapse.
