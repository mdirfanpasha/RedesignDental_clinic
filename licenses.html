Section 01

1. Healthcare Establishment Licensing

Redesign Dental Clinics is a licensed healthcare establishment authorized to deliver multi-specialty dental and maxillofacial care within the state of Telangana, India. All operations comply with the Clinical Establishments (Registration and Regulation) Act and local municipal healthcare authorities.

Our facilities at Banjara Hills and associated clinical locations maintain active clearances across:

  • State Dental Council Registration: Licensed under the Telangana State Dental Council with certified clinical infrastructure.
  • Biomedical Waste Management: Certified by the Telangana State Pollution Control Board (TSPCB) for hazardous clinical waste separation, handling, and daily disposal through authorized medical incinerator partners.
  • Fire & Building Life Safety: Compliant with municipal fire prevention and emergency evacuation norms.
Section 02

2. Practitioner Qualifications & DCI Compliance

Every clinical practitioner, specialist, surgeon, and hygienist operating at Redesign Dental Clinics holds valid registrations in good standing with the Dental Council of India (DCI) and the respective State Dental Councils.

Leadership Registration: Dr. Suhail (Chief Doctor)

Dr. Suhail possesses certified post-graduate specialization credentials (MDS) in Orthodontics and Dento-Facial Orthopedics. He is an active member of the Indian Orthodontic Society (IOS) and maintains continuous accreditation with state regulatory bodies. Practitioner registration details can be formally verified through our front desk or via official state council directories.

All visiting specialists (oral and maxillofacial surgeons, endodontists, implantologists, and pediatric dentists) undergo internal credential verification prior to patient consultations.

Section 03

3. Radiation Safety & Sterilization Accreditations

Patient safety and radiographic accuracy are governed by stringent regulatory guidelines:

  • AERB Clearance: Our high-frequency digital X-ray, panoramic OPG, and CBCT 3D scanning machinery operate under registration and compliance with the Atomic Energy Regulatory Board (AERB) of India, utilizing lead shielding and ALARA (As Low As Reasonably Achievable) radiation exposure parameters.
  • Class-B Multi-Cycle Autoclaving: All reusable instruments undergo a hospital-grade 5-stage decontamination protocol featuring medical-grade Class-B vacuum autoclaving, enzymatic cleaning, ultrasonic baths, and sterile vacuum-sealed pouching.
  • OSHA & CDC Guidelines: We adhere rigorously to Centers for Disease Control and OSHA infection-control and barrier-film protocols for dental operatories.

Section 06

6. Permitted & Restricted Usage

Visitors and patients are granted a limited, revocable, non-exclusive license to view, bookmark, and share links to our public informational pages for personal, non-commercial healthcare research.

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

  • Automated scraping, harvesting, or reverse engineering of patient portals, doctor profiles, or booking systems.
  • Using doctor headshots, case documentation, or clinic imagery on unauthorized third-party healthcare directories or marketing materials.
  • Deep-linking or presenting clinic assets inside iframes or deceptive wrapper applications.
