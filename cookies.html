01

What Are Cookies & Local Storage

Cookies are small, secure text files that websites store on your computer, tablet, or smartphone when you visit. They are standard across modern web applications and allow our platform to remember your browsing language, keep the appointment calendar responsive, and recognize you when you return.

Cookies cannot execute malicious code, deliver viruses, or read files stored on your hard drive.

02

Categories of Cookies We Use

We classify all cookies utilized on redesigndentalclinics.com into four distinct operational tiers:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Fundamental for basic page routing, secure token transmission, and appointment slot reservations. These cannot be switched off in our systems.
  • Functional & Preference Cookies: Retain selected language preferences (English, Telugu, Hindi, Arabic) across browsing sessions so you do not have to reset your choices on each visit.
  • Performance & Diagnostics Cookies: Help us measure load times, high-traffic service pages, and mobile responsiveness anonymously to optimize site speed.
  • Security & Bot Prevention: Google reCAPTCHA v3 tokens that protect our clinical appointment forms from automated spam submissions and DDoS exploits.

04

Why We Deploy These Technologies

Unlike commercial e-commerce websites, our use of cookies is solely clinical and functional. We deploy cookies to:

Patient Benefit: Enable seamless appointment bookings without repeated entries, prevent form spam that would block clinic scheduling, and display treatment guides in your native language.
05

Managing & Disabling Cookies

You hold complete control over your browser’s cookie preferences. Most modern web browsers allow you to view stored cookies, delete individual items, or block cookies altogether:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Third-party cookies.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions.

Note: If you disable strictly necessary cookies, certain interactive capabilities (such as automated slot validation on the booking calendar) may require manual telephone confirmation.

06

Third-Party Technologies

Our website integrates select secure third-party services:

  • Google Analytics: Configured with IP anonymization to evaluate page popularity and diagnose broken links without tracking personally identifiable medical details.
  • Google reCAPTCHA v3: Operates silently in the background to calculate risk scores and reject automated bots on appointment submission forms.
