Acceptance of Terms

By booking an appointment, browsing this website, or undergoing clinical consultations at Redesign Dental Clinics (Banjara Hills, Hyderabad), you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions. These terms establish a professional relationship based on trust, mutual respect, and patient safety.

02

Scope of Dental & Surgical Care

Redesign Dental Clinics provides specialized and general dental services, including Periodontics, Strategic Implantology, Orthodontics, Micro-Endodontics, Prosthodontics, and Cosmetic Smile Design under the clinical leadership of Dr. Suhail A. Syed and certified consulting specialists.

Information presented on this website (such as procedure overviews, blog posts, and smile gallery before-and-after results) is educational in nature. It is not an alternative to an in-person diagnostic examination by a licensed dental surgeon.

03

Appointment Scheduling & Cancellation Policy

To ensure zero waiting times and dedicated doctor focus for every patient:

  • Arrival Protocol: Patients are requested to arrive 10 minutes before their scheduled appointment slot for check-in and vital history updates.
  • Rescheduling & Cancellations: If you need to reschedule or cancel a visit, please inform our front desk at least 4 hours in advance via telephone or WhatsApp (+91 8179738737) so the reserved surgical slot may be offered to waiting emergency patients.
  • Doctor Emergencies: In the rare event an urgent acute surgical trauma requires our chief surgeon’s immediate attention, our team will proactively inform you and reschedule your session at the earliest mutual convenience.
04

Diagnostic Consultations & Informed Consent

No clinical procedure, surgical intervention, or sedation is undertaken without thorough informed consent:

Prior to commencing any restorative, orthodontic, or implant procedure, your attending dentist will explain the diagnosis, treatment alternatives, anticipated longevity, potential biological risks, post-treatment care, and estimated fees. You will have full opportunity to ask questions before signing your consent form.

Patients are required to provide complete, truthful disclosure of their existing medical background, drug allergies, cardiovascular status, diabetes indices, and current prescription regimens.

05

Fees, Estimates & Transparent Billing

We maintain transparent pricing with no hidden facility charges:

  • All fee estimates provided in treatment roadmaps are detailed line-by-line (including laboratory charges for custom zirconia crowns, clear aligner fabrication, or bone grafts).
  • We accept major credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and verified zero-interest EMI healthcare financing options.
  • Itemized tax invoices are issued for every clinical visit and treatment session.
06

Acute Dental Emergencies Protocol

If you experience acute dental trauma, severe facial swelling, broken teeth, or unmanageable dental pain during operating hours, contact our clinic immediately via telephone (+91 7780-245-307) for priority same-day triage.

For life-threatening emergencies occurring outside clinic hours (such as airway compromise or heavy facial bleeding from major trauma), proceed immediately to the nearest hospital emergency department.
