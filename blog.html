Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Care

How often should I visit a dentist in Banjara Hills?

For many people, a routine dental check-up every six months is a useful guideline, but the ideal frequency depends on your oral health, dental history, and individual needs. Regular dental visits can help identify problems early and support long-term oral health.
Do you provide emergency dental care in Hyderabad?

Yes. Redesign Dental Clinics provides emergency dental care for urgent dental concerns such as severe toothache, dental injuries, swelling, or other unexpected problems. Contact the clinic as soon as possible so the condition can be assessed and the appropriate treatment can be recommended.
Are dental treatments painful?

Comfort is an important part of dental care at Redesign Dental Clinics. Many procedures are performed using local anaesthesia or other appropriate techniques to help minimize discomfort. The experience can vary depending on the treatment and individual sensitivity, and our team will explain what to expect before treatment.
What dental services does Redesign Dental Clinics provide?

Redesign Dental Clinics offers a wide range of dental services in Banjara Hills, Hyderabad, including preventive dental care, teeth cleaning, teeth whitening, root canal treatment, dental implants, crowns, dentures and bridges, gum treatment, oral surgery, clear and invisible braces, veneers, pediatric dental care, and emergency dental care.
Do you offer cosmetic dentistry and smile-enhancing treatments?

Yes. Redesign Dental Clinics offers cosmetic dental treatments designed to improve the appearance of your smile, including teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, teeth reshaping, and smile makeover treatments. Your dentist can recommend options based on your teeth, oral health, and aesthetic goals.
Do you provide root canal treatment in Banjara Hills?

Yes. Redesign Dental Clinics provides root canal treatment and other endodontic care for teeth affected by infection or damage. Depending on the condition, treatment may include conventional root canal therapy, endodontic retreatment, or other appropriate procedures.
Do you provide dental implants in Hyderabad?

Yes. Redesign Dental Clinics offers dental implant treatment as part of its restorative dental services. Dental implants may be considered for replacing missing teeth, with the treatment plan depending on your oral health, bone condition, and individual dental needs.
Do you treat gum problems and periodontal conditions?

Yes. Our dental services include gum treatment and periodontal care such as scaling and root planing, non-surgical periodontal therapy, gum grafting, and periodontal pocket reduction when clinically appropriate.
Do you offer clear and invisible braces?

Redesign Dental Clinics provides clear and invisible orthodontic options for patients who want a more discreet approach to improving tooth alignment. Your dentist can assess your teeth and recommend an appropriate treatment option.
Where is Redesign Dental Clinics located?

Redesign Dental Clinics is located on the 6th Floor, Reliance Classic Enclave, Road no. 1, Banjara Hills, Hyderabad, Telangana - 500034. The clinic serves patients looking for professional dental care in Banjara Hills and surrounding areas of Hyderabad.
