Teeth Cleaning & Polishing

Professional cleaning that helps remove plaque, tartar, and surface buildup for healthier teeth and gums.

✦ Redesign Dental Clinics | Banjara Hills
Treatment Overview

What is Teeth Cleaning?

Professional teeth cleaning (scaling and polishing) is the foundation of preventive dental care. Even with diligent daily brushing and flossing, mineral-rich saliva causes dental plaque to harden into calculus (tartar) in hard-to-reach areas around and between teeth.

Ultrasonic scaling gently removes hardened deposits and bacterial biofilms without abrading tooth enamel, followed by prophylactic polishing to smooth surfaces and remove everyday surface stains.

Clinical Indications

When May This Treatment Be Recommended?

Teeth Cleaning may be considered for patients experiencing any of the following dental indications or treatment goals:

Routine preventive maintenance recommended every 6 months for all adults and children
Visible yellow or brown tartar deposits along the gumline
Superficial surface stains from coffee, tea, red wine, or tobacco
Mild gingival bleeding or tenderness when brushing
Persistent bad breath caused by plaque accumulation
