Solutions for Loose Dentures

Explore options designed to improve stability and comfort for patients experiencing movement with traditional dentures.

✦ Redesign Dental Clinics | Banjara Hills
Treatment Overview

What is Solutions for Loose Dentures?

Traditional removable dentures can sometimes move during speaking or eating.

For suitable patients, dental implants may provide additional support for dentures or complete tooth replacement solutions.

Possible treatment options depend on bone support, oral health and individual treatment goals.

  • Specialist-led diagnosis & treatment planning

Clinical Indications

When May This Treatment Be Recommended?

Solutions for Loose Dentures may be considered for patients experiencing any of the following dental indications or treatment goals:

Dentures that slip, wobble, or float when speaking or laughing
Difficulty chewing firmer foods, fruits, or meats
Frequent sore spots and gum chafing under removable dentures
Reliance on messy, uncomfortable denture adhesive pastes
Lower dentures with little remaining ridge stability
Desire for a secure, confidence-restoring tooth replacement option
Treatment Advantages

Key Clinical & Aesthetic Benefits

Designed to deliver predictable, lasting oral health outcomes while prioritizing your personal comfort and smile aesthetics.

Secure Retention

Implants anchor dentures firmly, eliminating embarrassing slips and movements.

Restores Biting Strength

Significantly increases chewing force, allowing you to enjoy a complete, nutritious diet.

No Need for Adhesives

Snap-in or fixed connections eliminate the daily hassle of messy sticky creams.

Eliminates Gum Irritation

Reduces frictional movement, preventing painful sore spots and ulcers.

Preserves Jawbone

Implant posts stimulate the jawbone, slowing down progressive bone loss.

Multiple Treatment Options

Choices range from economical 2-implant snap-on overdentures to permanent fixed bridges.

Step-by-Step Care

Your Treatment Journey

A clear, structured overview of what you can expect during each phase of your care at Redesign Dental Clinics.

01

Comprehensive Denture Evaluation

Assessment of current dentures, gum health, bite relationship, and patient concerns.

02

3D CBCT Bone Scanning

Volumetric imaging maps available jawbone to identify ideal implant anchor locations.

03

Treatment Customization

Discussion of options: locator snap-on overdentures vs. bar-retained or fully fixed bridges.

04

Implant Placement

Two to four precision implants are placed in the jaw under comfortable local anesthesia.

05

Denture Adaptation

Your existing denture is modified or a new custom prosthesis is fabricated with matching snap-in attachments.

06

Final Snap-In Fitting & Review

Attachments are engaged for instant, rock-solid retention and complete chewing comfort.
