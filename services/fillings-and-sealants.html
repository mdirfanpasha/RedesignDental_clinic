Treatment Overview

What is Fillings & Sealants?

Dental fillings and sealants are essential treatments for repairing tooth decay and protecting deep fissures from bacterial invasion.

We use advanced composite resins that bond directly to natural tooth structure and match your exact tooth shade, eliminating dark silver amalgam fillings. Dental sealants provide a thin, protective barrier over deep grooves on chewing surfaces.

Clinical Indications

When May This Treatment Be Recommended?

Fillings & Sealants may be considered for patients experiencing any of the following dental indications or treatment goals:

Teeth with active dental decay or cavities
Chipped, worn, or fractured enamel
Deep grooves and pits on molars prone to plaque entrapment (ideal for sealants)
Replacement of old, leaking, or unsightly metal amalgam fillings
Minor cosmetic contouring of rough tooth edges

Step-by-Step Care

Your Treatment Journey

A clear, structured overview of what you can expect during each phase of your care at Redesign Dental Clinics.

01

Tooth Examination & Color Matching

The decayed area is assessed and an exact composite shade is selected.

02

Gentle Decay Removal

Decayed tooth structure is conservatively cleared under local anesthesia.

03

Enamel Conditioning & Bonding

An etching gel and adhesive bonding agent prepare the tooth surface at a microscopic level.

04

Layered Composite Placement

Resin is applied in anatomical increments to recreate natural tooth cusps.

05

LED Light Polymerization

High-intensity blue LED light cures and hardens the composite within seconds.

06

Bite Check & High-Gloss Polish

The restoration is shaped, bite harmony verified, and polished to a lifelike luster.
