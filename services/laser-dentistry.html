Laser Gum Treatment

Advanced laser-assisted periodontal treatment designed to support precise treatment of gum disease.

✦ Redesign Dental Clinics | Banjara Hills
Treatment Overview

What is Laser Gum Treatment?

Laser-assisted gum treatment may be considered as part of periodontal care for suitable patients.

Advanced periodontal disease can create spaces between the gums and teeth where bacteria and infection can develop. Laser technology may be used in appropriate clinical situations as part of a treatment plan.

Suitability depends on the patient's periodontal condition and professional evaluation.

Clinical Indications

When May This Treatment Be Recommended?

Laser Gum Treatment may be considered for patients experiencing any of the following dental indications or treatment goals:

Patients with moderate to advanced periodontal pockets
Patients seeking a minimally invasive alternative to traditional scalpel surgery
Gums that have not responded adequately to conventional scaling alone
Patients concerned about post-surgical gum recession
Medically compromised patients requiring minimized bleeding

Step-by-Step Care

Your Treatment Journey

A clear, structured overview of what you can expect during each phase of your care at Redesign Dental Clinics.

01

Diagnostic Periodontal Assessment

Full-mouth charting measures pocket depths and identifies areas suitable for laser treatment.

02

Local Anesthesia

Gentle computerized numbing ensures the treatment area is completely comfortable.

03

Laser Decontamination

A tiny laser fiber is inserted into the pocket to vaporize diseased tissue and bacteria.

04

Ultrasonic Root Debridement

Calculus deposits on the root surface are removed with precision ultrasonic tips.

05

Laser Clot Formation

A second laser pass seals the pocket with a stable fibrin clot to protect the area during healing.

06

Follow-Up & Maintenance

Regular evaluations monitor pocket reduction and periodontal stability.
